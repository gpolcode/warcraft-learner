# Domain glossary

Canonical terms for warcraft-learner's domain. Code, findings, and docs use these words exactly.

## Cadence

The benched timing pattern of a pressable ability across the top parses: when it is first cast, the gaps between casts, how many uses a fight yields, and which casts the top parses hold for a later window. A cadence is measured per ability per encounter and is what a player's own casts are coached against.

- **Majority hold**: an ability's holds are coached when at least half of the parses that press it hold it. Ties count as holds.
- **Use share**: the fraction of benched parses that pressed the ability at all. An ability below half adoption is planned, not coached.

## Voice

The per-slice wording a shared coaching message is rendered in: the noun for one press ("cast" for rotation, "use" for defensives) and the verb that opens a remedy ("Press", "Use"). The voice changes the words of a finding, never its math.
