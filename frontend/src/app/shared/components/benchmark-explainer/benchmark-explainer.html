A parse is one player's ranked Warcraft Logs record of a single kill. warcraft-learner measures you against the top parses for your spec on this boss, Mythic only.

It reads the 10 highest ranked of those logs, or fewer while a boss is new, and re-checks them every hour. Every comparison, plan and target on these cards comes from that group.

"Top raiders" means the players behind those logs, and "the plan" is what they did. A percentage counts them, so "60% of top parsers" is 6 of the 10.

In a measured cell your pull comes first. The number after it is what you were judged against: what top raiders did, the target to aim for, or how many casts were judged.
